📖 About hwplug

Welcome to hwplug – your trusted academic support platform designed specifically for UK students. We understand that balancing schoolwork, extracurricular activities, and personal life can be overwhelming. That's why we created hwplug: to provide reliable, affordable, and efficient homework completion services.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to help students manage their academic workload more effectively. We believe that every student deserves access to support tools that can help them succeed without burning out. By leveraging advanced automation technology, we make homework completion faster and more accessible.

💡 Our Vision: To become the UK's leading academic support platform, helping thousands of students achieve better work-life balance while maintaining their academic performance.

🤖 What We Do

hwplug specializes in automated homework completion for popular UK educational platforms:

  • Sparx Maths – Complete maths homework assignments across all topics and difficulty levels
  • Sparx Reader – Answer reading comprehension questions using AI-powered analysis
  • Seneca Learning – Complete lessons and quizzes across multiple subjects
  • Educake (PiXL) – Handle science homework for Biology, Chemistry, and Physics

💡 How We Started

hwplug was founded by students who experienced firsthand the stress of managing multiple homework platforms. We noticed that many students were spending hours each week on repetitive online homework tasks, leaving little time for deeper learning, hobbies, or rest.

We developed an automated solution that could handle these routine assignments efficiently, giving students back their time. What started as a small project has grown into a trusted service used by students across the UK.
