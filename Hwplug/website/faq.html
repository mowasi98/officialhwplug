🏠 General Questions

What is hwplug?

hwplug is an automated homework completion service for UK students. We use advanced automation technology to complete assignments on popular educational platforms including Sparx Maths, Sparx Reader, Seneca Learning, and Educate (PiXL). Our service helps students manage their workload and save time.

How does hwplug work?

After you purchase a service, you provide your school login credentials. Our automation system securely logs into your account, navigates to your homework assignments, and completes them automatically. The process typically takes a few hours depending on the assignment complexity. You'll receive an email confirmation when complete.

Is this service legal?

Yes, our service is legal in the UK. However, using automated homework completion may violate your school's academic integrity policies. You are responsible for ensuring compliance with your school's rules. We recommend using our service as a study aid and reviewing completed work to learn from it.

🔒 Safety & Security

Is my information safe?

Yes. We use SSL encryption for all data transmission. Your login credentials are only stored temporarily during homework completion and are deleted immediately after. We never share your information with third parties. All payments are processed through Stripe's secure payment gateway, and we never see or store your card details.

Will my school know I used this service?

Our automation mimics natural student behavior, including realistic typing speeds and answer times. However, we cannot guarantee complete anonymity. Educational platforms may have detection systems, and your school may have policies against using such services. Use responsibly and at your own discretion.

What happens to my password after you're done?

Your password is deleted from our systems immediately after homework completion. We do not store credentials in any database or log file. For added security, you can change your password after we complete your homework.

⚙️ Service Details

How long does it take to complete my homework?

Completion times vary by platform: Sparx Reader (1-3 hours), Sparx Maths (2-6 hours), Seneca (2-5 hours), and Educate (3-6 hours). Most assignments are completed within 24 hours. Complex or lengthy assignments may take longer. We guarantee completion within 48 hours or you receive a full refund.

What is the slot system?

Each product has 3 regular slots per day at £2 each. When these fill up, 5 extra slots become available at £3 each. This system prevents overload and ensures quality service. All slots reset daily at midnight UK time. You can check real-time availability on our homepage.

What if all slots are full?

You have two options: (1) Purchase an extra slot at £3 if available, or (2) Wait until midnight UK time when slots reset to standard £2 pricing. Extra slots are limited to 5 per product, so they can also sell out during busy periods.

Can I buy multiple services at once?

Yes! You can purchase services for different platforms in separate transactions. Each platform is handled independently. However, you cannot buy multiple slots for the same platform simultaneously - each order is processed one at a time.

Most homework is completed within 24 hours, but we guarantee completion within 48 hours. If you have a tight deadline (less than 48 hours), contact us on Snapchat BEFORE ordering to confirm we can meet it. We'll prioritize urgent orders when possible.

Can you do my homework right now?

Our automation processes orders in queue order. While we can't guarantee immediate start, most orders begin within 1-4 hours of purchase. If you need urgent service, contact us on Snapchat and we'll do our best to prioritize your order if capacity allows.
