💳 Refund Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

At hwplug, we strive to provide reliable and high-quality homework completion services. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued and the process for requesting them.

1. General Refund Policy

Due to the nature of our service (immediate homework completion), we generally do not offer refunds once work has begun. However, we understand that issues can arise, and we handle refund requests on a case-by-case basis.

2. Eligible Refund Scenarios

You may be eligible for a full or partial refund in the following circumstances:

Full Refund Eligibility:

  • Technical Failure: Our automation system fails to complete your homework due to technical issues on our end
  • Service Not Delivered: Homework not completed within 48 hours without prior communication or explanation
  • Incorrect Credentials: We discover that the credentials you provided don't work, and we inform you before starting work
  • Platform Unavailable: The educational platform is down or inaccessible, preventing completion
  • Duplicate Charge: You were accidentally charged twice for the same order

Partial Refund Eligibility:

  • Incomplete Work: Homework is partially completed but not finished due to unforeseen issues
  • Significant Delays: Completion takes significantly longer than estimated (beyond 48 hours) due to our error

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Incorrect Credentials Provided: You provided wrong username, password, or school name
  • Account Issues: Your educational platform account is locked, suspended, or restricted
  • Change of Mind: You decide you no longer want the service after work has begun
  • School Detection: Your school detects or questions the completed homework (we cannot control school policies)
  • Homework Already Complete: The homework was already completed before we accessed your account
  • Platform Changes: The educational platform changes its system in a way that temporarily affects our automation
  • Normal Completion Time: Homework takes the typical estimated time (not a valid reason for refund)

4. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Contact Us Promptly: Submit your refund request within 48 hours of your order via Snapchat (@homework5003)
  2. Provide Order Details: Include your order ID, product name, and reason for refund request
  3. Explain the Issue: Clearly describe what went wrong and why you believe a refund is warranted
  4. Wait for Review: We will review your request within 24-48 hours and respond with our decision
  5. Refund Processing: If approved, refunds are processed within 5-10 business days to your original payment method

💡 Tip: Before requesting a refund, contact us to see if we can resolve the issue. Many problems can be fixed quickly, and we're committed to customer satisfaction.

5. Refund Timeline

  • Request Review: 24-48 hours from submission
  • Refund Processing: 1-3 business days after approval
  • Bank Processing: 3-7 business days (depends on your bank)
  • Total Time: Typically 5-10 business days from approval to funds in your account

6. Chargebacks and Disputes

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute without first contacting us:

  • Your account will be immediately suspended pending investigation
  • You may be banned from using our service in the future
  • We will provide evidence to your bank/payment provider showing service delivery

Please contact us first before initiating a chargeback. We're committed to resolving issues fairly and quickly.

7. Slot Reservation Cancellations

If you reserve a slot but don't complete payment:

  • Your reservation expires after 15 minutes automatically
  • No charge is made, so no refund is needed
  • You can reserve again if slots are still available

8. Extra Slot Refunds

Extra slots (£3) follow the same refund policy as regular slots (£2). The higher price does not affect refund eligibility or process.

9. Contact for Refund Requests

To request a refund or discuss any payment issues:

Snapchat: @homework5003

Please include:

  • Your order ID (found in your confirmation email)
  • Product name (Sparx Maths, Sparx Reader, etc.)
  • Date of purchase
  • Detailed explanation of the issue
  • Any relevant screenshots or evidence

⚠️ Important: Refund requests submitted after 48 hours may not be considered. Please contact us promptly if you experience any issues with our service.

10. Fair Use Policy

We reserve the right to deny refunds to users who:

  • Make excessive or fraudulent refund requests
  • Violate our Terms of Service
  • Provide false information in refund requests
  • Abuse our customer support or refund process

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. Continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
