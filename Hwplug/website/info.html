Sparx Reader

What is Sparx Reader? Sparx Reader is an online reading comprehension platform that helps students improve literacy skills. It presents passages followed by multiple-choice questions testing understanding, vocabulary, and inference.

How we help: Our AI-powered system reads passages and answers comprehension questions with high accuracy. We use advanced natural language processing to understand context and select correct answers.

Seneca Learning

What is Seneca? Seneca is a free learning platform covering multiple subjects including Science, English, Maths, and Humanities. It uses bite-sized lessons with interactive questions to help students learn and revise.

How we help: Our system completes Seneca homework assignments across all subjects. We navigate through lessons, answer quiz questions, and ensure all assigned work is marked as complete.

Educate (PiXL)

What is Educate? Educate (formerly PiXL) is a comprehensive science learning platform used by many UK secondary schools. It covers Biology, Chemistry, and Physics with interactive lessons and assessments.

How we help: Our automation handles Educate science homework across all three sciences. We complete interactive activities, answer questions, and work through practical scenarios.

Typical completion time: 3-6 hours for comprehensive science assignments. Complex practical assessments may require additional time for accuracy.
