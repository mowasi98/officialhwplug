- High CPM rates
- 100% fill rate

### 2. 🔔 Push Notifications

- Recurring revenue

### 3. 📱 In-Page Push (Banner-style)

### User Experience:

1. **User visits your site** → MultiTag loads

3. **User may see notification prompt** → "Allow notifications?"
4. **In-page ads may appear** → Small notification-style banners
5. **PropellerAds optimizes** → Shows best format for that user
